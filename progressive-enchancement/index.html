Thoughts on Progressive Enhancement

Before reading the article that we were assigned, I didn't know much about progressive enhancement. Upon reading it, I found that not only did the idea resonate with me heavily, but I was already familiar with it due to this class. Progressive enhancement is an approach to web design which focuses on the content created rather than the browser. It also emphasizes the importance of accessibility and functionality. This is contrasted against the graceful degradation approach which focuses on building for the most advanced browsers, with consequences for browsers which have issues with accessibility. This is crucial to not only consumer based websites but also work related ones aswell. This isn't to say that there are no potential flaws with the approach, just that it answers many problems that web development can face.

After reading this article I searched for more information about progressive enhancement and found this article. In this article by geeks for geeks they go into the philosophy and how the approach relates to the use of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. They do this not only by describing the approach but also giving examples of its use across the three coding languages. This gave me more insight and helped me understand why the approach is important to modern web design. In my opinion, the approach is crucial because it ensures that the web is moving towards the goal of being universally accessible. This accessibility has become even more important as the web has scaled in size and user base, as more people rely on the site for work, commerce, and socialization.
